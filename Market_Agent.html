⚡ Run Controls
Run Agent
Runs the full analysis — fetches live market data, evaluates every candidate against all quality gates, updates holdings and picks, and refreshes this dashboard.
Pre Market
Checks yesterday's recommendations against this morning's pre-market prices, scans leading stocks in each sector for new opportunities, and sends a summary to Telegram before the market opens.
Day Insight
Looks at what is happening with a specific stock right now — whether the move is coming from that company alone, its sector, or the overall market — and matches today's news to the exact timing of the price change. Sent directly to Telegram.
📋 Stock Universe
Rebuilds the 300-stock candidate master list from ALL NYSE + NASDAQ common stock above a $1B market cap floor — not just S&P 500 constituents, which structurally exclude foreign companies like Shell or BP regardless of size. Selected across three market-cap tiers with protected budgets — 5% ($1B–$5B), 25% ($5B–$13B), 70% ($13B+) — so small and mid-cap opportunities always get a real, guaranteed share rather than being crowded out by large-caps. Same relative-to-sector quality scoring either way (revenue growth, margins, low beta, FCF yield, low debt) — the tiers only change how many slots each size band gets, not how a stock is judged within its band. Replaces the old approach of Claude re-inventing ~52 tickers from memory every run, which meant a stock passing every gate today could simply vanish tomorrow. This list only changes when you click refresh. Realistically takes 30-90+ minutes now (checking thousands of tickers, not hundreds) — safe to close this page or refresh your browser: it keeps running on this laptop as long as the start.sh terminal stays open, and this page shows where it stands whenever you check back.
⏰ US Market Hours (Oman Time)
